# BooStudy tokens

The executable source is `static/css/boostudy-design-system.css`. Tokens are semantic: pages consume `--bs-*` values instead of inventing local colors, radii or shadows.

Categories: page/surface backgrounds, text, borders, semantic accents, spacing, radii, elevation, typography, motion and breakpoints.

The system intentionally uses purple/indigo as the primary brand accent while keeping success, info, warning and danger semantic. Bento separation is primarily `gap + border + background + radius`; elevation is secondary.
